Meet the Leadership Team

Dedicated professionals committed to delivering safe, high-quality, person-centred care.

Ravindra Grewal

Managing Director

Ravindra Grewal is the Managing Director of CS Hub Ltd., a registered pharmacist, and holds a Level 7 Diploma in Health and Social Care Management. With over 20 years of experience in health and social care, he provides strategic leadership and oversees the quality and safety of services.

He is committed to delivering compassionate, person-centred care that respects each individual’s needs, promotes independence, and ensures families feel listened to, involved, and valued.

Garikai Mungwariri

Operations Manager & CQC Registered Manager

Garikai Mungwariri brings over 15 years of leadership experience in health and social care. As Operations Manager and CQC Registered Manager at CS Hub Ltd, he combines compassionate leadership with a strong focus on service quality, compliance, and continuous improvement.

He is dedicated to delivering safe, high-quality, person-centred care. He has successfully led services to achieve CQC "Good" ratings and specialises in governance, safeguarding, compliance, staff development, and service improvement.

Garikai holds a Level 5 Diploma in Leadership for Health and Social Care, an MSc in Global Health Management, and a BSc (Hons). He is passionate about creating environments where people feel safe, valued, and empowered to live fulfilling lives.

Puja Lund

Administrative Support

Puja Lund plays a key administrative role at CS Hub Ltd., supporting the smooth, compliant, and efficient day-to-day running of services. She manages the end-to-end referral process, maintains accurate care records, and regularly reviews documentation on Nourish to ensure information is up to date and meets regulatory standards.

In her vital support role, Puja coordinates essential information across internal teams, families, and relevant professionals with confidentiality and care. Her strong organisational skills and attention to detail help ensure the team has the information they need to deliver high-quality care, and that the people we support feel well looked after.

Jigyasa Pahwa

HR Officer

With more than 13 years of experience in human resources, Jigyasa Pahwa supports CS Hub Ltd as HR Officer. She holds an MA in International Business Management and is currently undertaking CIPD Level 7.

Jigyasa brings experience across a broad range of HR functions, supporting employee relations, people processes, HR analytics, policy implementation, and workforce administration. She helps ensure HR information is accurate, processes are applied consistently, and managers and employees receive timely and practical support.

She takes an organised and people-focused approach to HR, combining attention to detail, clear communication, and continuous professional development to support the effective running of the organisation.

Tanya Thampy

Care Manager

Tanya Thampy is a Care Manager at CS Hub Ltd., with specialist experience in Positive Behaviour Support and compliance. She holds a Master’s degree in Psychology and has experience supporting children and adults with autism, ADHD, learning disabilities, and complex behavioural needs.

Her background includes developing Positive Behaviour Support plans, conducting functional behaviour assessments, supporting staff training, and maintaining high standards of care, governance, and compliance.
