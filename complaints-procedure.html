Complaints Procedure

1. Our Commitment

At Care Solutions Hub, we are committed to providing high-quality, person-centred care. We welcome all feedback, including complaints, as it gives us the opportunity to learn, improve, and ensure we consistently meet the high standards we set for ourselves and the people we support.

2. Who Can Make a Complaint?

A complaint can be made by anyone who receives support from us, their family members, advocates, or any other person affected by our services.

3. How to Make a Complaint

You can make a complaint verbally, in writing, or via email. We encourage you to raise any concerns as soon as possible so we can address them promptly.

  • By Phone: +44 1892 342 712
  • By Email: info@caresol.co.uk
  • In Writing: Registered Manager, Care Solutions Hub, Pluto House, 6 Vale Avenue, Tunbridge Wells, TN1 1DJ

4. Our Process for Handling Complaints

  1. Acknowledgement: We will acknowledge your complaint in writing within 3 working days of receiving it.
  2. Investigation: A designated manager will thoroughly investigate the concerns raised. We may contact you during this period to clarify details or ask for more information.
  3. Resolution: We aim to provide a full written response detailing the outcome of the investigation and any actions taken within 28 working days. If the investigation requires more time, we will keep you informed of the progress and expected completion date.
  4. Action and Learning: We will implement any necessary changes to our practices to prevent similar issues from arising in the future.

5. Escalating Your Complaint

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of our investigation, you have the right to escalate your complaint further. You can contact the Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman (LGSCO) or your local authority commissioning team.

You may also share your feedback with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). While the CQC does not investigate individual complaints, they use this information to monitor and inspect services.

6. Confidentiality

All complaints are treated with the strictest confidence. Information will only be shared on a need-to-know basis to effectively investigate and resolve the issue.

Need to speak with us?

If you prefer to discuss your concerns informally first, please don't hesitate to reach out to our team.
