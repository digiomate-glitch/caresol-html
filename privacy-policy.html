Privacy Policy and Procedure

Company: CS Hub Limited (Pluto House, 6 Vale Avenue, Tunbridge Wells, TN1 1DJ)

Last Reviewed: 21 Jan 2026

Next Review: Annual (or as needs require)

1. Purpose

To support the human rights of the Individuals and ensure that CS Hub Limited complies with legislation and regulation in relation to the privacy of individuals. This policy covers the day-to-day considerations of ensuring privacy whilst supporting individuals.

This policy should be read in conjunction with the suite of associated policies and procedures at CS Hub Limited such as UK GDPR in relation to data protection and information sharing.

2. Objectives

  • To reinforce the requirement, in accordance with professional codes of conduct, to ensure that privacy is maintained as per the Individuals's wishes.
  • To provide clarity and standard practice recommendations to staff at CS Hub Limited to ensure that Individuals have dignity and privacy when they need and want it.
  • To ensure that Individuals', employees' and stakeholders' personal data and information is treated with the same level of protection around privacy.

3. Policy Statement

CS Hub Limited recognises the right of Individuals to be left alone, undisturbed and free from intrusion and public attention. The Individuals also have a right to privacy with regard to their personal affairs, data, and their belongings.

Staff will adhere to the human rights of individuals and work in accordance with professional codes of conduct, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) guidance, UK GDPR and Data Protection Policies and Procedures at CS Hub Limited.

Intentional breaches of privacy will be fully investigated with appropriate bodies informed and lessons learnt.

4. Procedure

4.1 Care Planning at the Start of a Service

  • The needs of the Individuals regarding privacy will always be considered during the pre-service visit assessment stage to ensure that CS Hub Limited can effectively meet the person's needs.
  • This assessment will include what information about them can be shared and with whom, including formal consent to share information.
  • Preferred wishes must be communicated to other relevant staff at CS Hub Limited.
  • Where possible, the pre-service assessment process will be completed in a private area where the Individuals can feel able to discuss areas of their care needs.

4.2 Individual Rights

  • Individuals' choices in relation to privacy will be respected at all times and all information relating to them will be treated in a confidential manner.

4.3 Staff Expectation, Behaviour and Professionalism

  • Staff will follow professional codes of conduct as well as operational policies and procedures when considering privacy.
  • Staff will only discuss Individuals in the work environment if it is for the purpose of assessment, management and evaluation of care.
  • Staff will not discuss any aspect of the Individuals's care outside of the work environment.

4.4 Records Management

  • Records will be designed, used, and stored in a manner which assures privacy.
  • Records will only be made available to the Individuals’s family and main Care Worker according to their wishes or if information requests fulfil the legal requirements.

4.5 Personal Care and Privacy

  • Particular attention will be given to preserving privacy during the use of bathrooms, toilets and when supporting any aspect of personal care.
  • Staff will ensure, with Individuals consent, that curtains/blinds/doors are closed in order to ensure privacy.
  • Staff will always knock on the Individuals's door and await a response before entering the room. Privacy will be respected around the Individuals's home at all times.

4.6 Breach in Privacy

Any breach in the privacy of the Individuals will be considered a serious event. The incident will be fully investigated in accordance with UK GDPR. Disciplinary action will be taken where the incident is considered to have been caused with intent.

5. Capacity and Privacy

The same rights of privacy apply to people who are proven to lack capacity, therefore staff must do the following:

  • Establish any previously expressed views or wishes of the individual regarding privacy from family and others.
  • Observe Individuals' behaviours to identify what the preferences may be for that person wishing to have privacy.
  • Continue to follow the core principles and practices as detailed within this policy, if deemed to be in the best interests of the Individuals to do so (in accordance with the Mental Capacity Act).

6. Key Facts - People Affected by The Service

  • You have full rights to privacy. Staff will discuss what your wishes are around this and what that means for you.
  • Information about you will only be shared with your permission, or if you are unable to give permission, when it is deemed to be in your best interest to do so.
  • Staff supporting you will respect your privacy wishes and support you in providing environments that are private.
