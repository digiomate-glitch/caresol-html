Outreach Support

We offer flexible, person-centred outreach support for individuals with Learning Disabilities and/or Autism Spectrum Disorder. Our outreach services are designed to promote your independence at home and help you actively participate in your community.

Flexible support, built around your goals

Our outreach support is tailored to your unique needs, with a focus on achieving positive outcomes and enhancing your independence. We work closely with you and your support network to ensure that the services provided are personalised and aligned with your goals. Outreach can sit alongside family care, complement supported living, or stand alone — whatever level of support fits your life right now.

Support that goes where life happens

Everyday Skills

Building confidence with budgeting, cooking, travel and independent living skills.

Community Access

Getting out and about — clubs, classes, appointments and social activities.

Communication

Support to build confidence expressing needs, wishes and preferences.

Personal Goals

Whatever matters to you — work, hobbies, relationships — we help you get there.

Hours and focus, set by you

Outreach support is flexible by design. Together we agree how many hours suit you, which goals to focus on first, and how we'll know it's working — then we review and adjust as your confidence grows.

  • Sessions scheduled around your routine, not a fixed rota.
  • Consistent support workers who get to know you properly.
  • Regular reviews with you and your support network.
